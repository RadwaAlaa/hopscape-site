Hopscape Support

Help for the Hopscape arcade game on iPhone and iPad.

Contact
Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Email eng_radwa_alaa@yahoo.com. Please mention your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

How to play

  • Tap (or swipe up) to hop forward.
  • Swipe left, right or down to hop sideways or back.
  • Grass is safe, and trees block your way.
  • On roads, dodge the cars and trucks.
  • On rivers, land on logs and ride them. Don't fall in the water or drift off the edge.
  • On railway tracks, a flashing red signal means a train is coming.
  • Keep moving. The screen creeps forward, and if you fall behind, the run ends.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock new characters?
Collect coins during your runs. On the start screen, swipe or use the arrows to browse characters, then tap "Unlock" when you have enough coins.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. Hopscape has no ads and nothing to buy. Every character is earned by playing.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. The game works completely offline.
Where is my progress saved?
Your best score, coins and unlocked characters are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this progress, and it doesn't move to a new device.
Does Hopscape collect any data?
No. See the privacy policy.
