Privacy Policy

Hopscape · Last updated October 30, 2026

In short: Hopscape does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no network access.

Information we collect

None. Hopscape does not ask for, collect or send any personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or usage data.

Data stored on your device

To keep your progress between sessions, the game saves your best score, your coin total, the characters you've unlocked and the character you picked. This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and you can remove it at any time by deleting the app.

Third parties

Hopscape contains no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

Children's privacy

Because Hopscape collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Contacting us by email

If you choose to email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we don't share them with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
